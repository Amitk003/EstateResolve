# Executor Guidelines

## Overview

As executor, your role is to manage the estate of the deceased person. This includes identifying assets, paying debts, filing taxes, and distributing remaining assets to beneficiaries.

## Key steps

1. Locate the will and file it with the probate court
2. Get certified copies of the death certificate (get 10-15 copies)
3. Notify banks, credit card companies, and other institutions of the death
4. Identify and secure all assets
5. Pay outstanding debts from the estate
6. File final income tax returns
7. Distribute remaining assets to beneficiaries
8. Close the estate

## What to send to each institution

- A certified copy of the death certificate
- Proof of your appointment as executor (Letters Testamentary)
- A formal request to freeze or close the account
- Your contact information

## Document types you may encounter

- Bank statements - record the account number and balance
- Credit card bills - record the outstanding balance
- Utility bills - record the account and any outstanding amount
- Insurance policies - record the policy number
- Property tax bills - record the parcel number
- Investment statements - record the holdings and value
- Legal notices - record the case number and court
